Welcome

to


Christ Apostolic Church

Chapel of Glory District Headquarter

W ith much joy in our heart, we welcome you to Christ Apostolic Church, Chapel of Glory District Headquarter official website. We are delighted to have you join us in this divine assembly, where the unchanging Word of God is preached in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our mission is to preach the total gospel of Jesus Christ to all nations, demonstrating the power of the Holy Spirit, raising a Holy and prayerful people, and building the body of Christ through evangelism, discipleship, and the teaching of God's unchanging Word.

Your visit here is not by accident - we believe God has ordered your steps, and we trust that you will encounter His presence and power. We invite you to connect with us through our services, teachings, and prayer meetings, and to share your testimonies of God's mighty works in your life.

Welcome once again, and remain blessed in Jesus' name!

-Pastor M. Adebiyi.
