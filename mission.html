Our Vision and Mission

Our mission is to lead people into a deeper knowledge of God through an in-depth study of His Word, prevailing prayer, and Spirit-filled worship. We are committed to raising believers who are firmly rooted in the truth of Scripture, strengthened by a life of prayer, and transformed through genuine worship. By equipping and nurturing God's people, we seek to build a vibrant community of disciples who will reflect Christ in their daily lives, impact their generation, and advance the Kingdom of God on earth.

Our vision is to faithfully preach the uncompromising Word of God with power and truth, reaching people of all nations and all generations with the saving message of Jesus Christ. We are committed to winning souls for Christ, raising disciples who will live by the Word, and sending them forth as witnesses to transform lives, families, and communities. Our pursuit is the advancement of the Kingdom of God on earth, as we work to reveal His love, righteousness, and glory in every sphere of life. We envision a world that experiences spiritual re-awakening, where hearts are revived, the church is empowered, and nations are aligned to the will of God. Through prayer, evangelism, teaching, and kingdom service, we strive to see the light of Christ shine in every corner of the earth until His glory covers the world as the waters cover the sea.


Our Church Tenets

  1. 1. The Unity of the God-Head and the Trinity of Persons Therein.
  2. 2. The Utter Depravity of Human Nature; the Necessity for Repentance and Regeneration and the Eternal Doom of the Finally Impenitent.
  3. 3. The Virgin Birth, Sinless Life, Atoning Death, Triumphant Resurrection, Ascension and Abiding Intercession of our Lord Jesus Christ; His Second Coming and Millennial Reign on Earth.
  4. 4. Justification and Sanctification of the Believers Through the Finished Work of Christ.
  5. 5. The Baptism of the Holy Ghost for Believers With Signs Following.
  6. 6. The Nine Gifts of the Holy Ghost for Edification, Exhortation and Comfort of the Church, Which is the Body of Christ.
  7. 7. The Sacraments of Baptism by Immersion and the Lord's Supper.
  8. 8. The Divine Inspiration and Authority of the Holy Scriptures.
  9. 9. Church Government by Apostles, Prophets, Evangelists, Pastors, Teachers, Elders and Deacons.
  10. 10. The Possibility of Falling from Grace.
  11. 11. The Obligatory Nature of Tithes and Offerings.
  12. 12. Divine Healing Through Obedience to the Command of our Lord Jesus Christ and Faith in His Name and Merit of His Blood for All Sicknesses, Diseases, and Infirmities. (Isaiah 53:5; Mark 16:18; James 5:14-18).
  13. 13. Faith in God, the Jehovah Jireh to Supply All Your Financial Needs Without Going Into Debt or Borrowing Money on Interest; and to be Content With Having Food and Raiment. Godliness With Contentment is a Great Gain, and the Love of Money is the Root of All Evils (Philippians 4:19; Romans 13:8; 1 Timothy 6:6-10; Psalm 15:5).
